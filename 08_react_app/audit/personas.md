# FOI Sentinel v2 — Audit Personas (2026-07-02)

Audit scope per operator: **the ENTIRE app from the end-user perspective.** End-user lens is primary; buyer/champion noted where they intersect an end-user screen.

## Lens 1 — End-user (PRIMARY): the FOI / SAR Officer
- **Role:** Information Rights / FOI Officer in a UK borough council. Processes FOI, EIR and SAR requests daily against a statutory 20-working-day clock.
- **Problem today:** requests arrive by email and get manually logged, classified, routed, researched, drafted and redacted. Slow, inconsistent, and SARs risk disclosing third-party personal data. No single view of what's due.
- **Goals:** see what's due and at-risk; triage inbound quickly; draft defensible responses grounded in the council's own figures + precedent; redact SARs safely (keep the requester's data, remove third parties'); never miss a deadline.
- **Key use cases:** (UC1) see workload/at-risk at a glance; (UC2) intake & auto-triage an inbound request; (UC3) open a case and get an AI-suggested, source-grounded answer; (UC4) benchmark the draft against peer disclosures; (UC5) redact a SAR document selectively; (UC6) find guidance/precedent.
- **Value:** faster cycle time, fewer missed deadlines, defensible + consistent responses, safe SAR redaction.

## Lens 2 — Economic buyer: Information Governance Manager / Head of Legal
- **Problem:** compliance exposure (ICO decisions, late responses, wrongful disclosure) + cost per FOI. Needs to justify spend.
- **Cares about (on end-user screens):** compliance % vs SLA, cost-effectiveness, auditability (who decided what), data honesty (no invented figures).
- **Key use cases:** (UC7) performance/compliance reporting; (UC8) cost-effectiveness; (UC9) evidence base / auditability.

## Lens 3 — Champion: IG Manager demoing to leadership
- **Cares about:** a clean narrative, an obvious "moment that matters" (grounded answer / live SAR redaction), nothing visibly broken or accidentally synthetic-looking.
- **Key use cases:** UC2→UC3→UC4 pipeline story; UC5 redaction studio as the wow moment.

## Success in one line
The officer can move an inbound request from inbox → triaged → grounded draft → peer-benchmarked → (for SARs) safely redacted, with deadlines and compliance visible throughout, and nothing on screen looks broken.
